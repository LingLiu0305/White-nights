An interactive adaptation of Fyodor Dostoevsky’s White Nights. Explore Petersburg, follow a dreamer’s footsteps, and read at your own pace. Your progress is saved on this device.

The city is a literary map, not a historical reconstruction. The English text is an original abridged adaptation informed by Constance Garnett’s translation, with the original ending preserved.

Illustrations: classroom Qwen Image API. City: procedural 3D.
Process, prompts and credits are documented in the repository.
